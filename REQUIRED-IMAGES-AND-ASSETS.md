## 📐 TECHNICAL SPECIFICATIONS

### **Image Optimization Requirements**

**For All Images**:
- Use WebP format with JPG/PNG fallback
- Compress to 85% quality or lower
- Serve responsive sizes (srcset)
- Lazy load below-the-fold images
- Use CDN for delivery (Cloudinary)

**Animation Files**:
- MP4 videos: H.264 codec, under 2MB
- Lottie animations: Optimize JSON, under 100KB
- GIFs: Only if Lottie not possible, heavily optimized

**Performance Targets**:
- Hero images: Load in under 1 second
- Below-fold images: Lazy loaded
- Animations: Start on viewport entry
- Total page weight: Under 3MB

## 📝 NOTES FOR DESIGNERS

1. **Real over Stock**: Use actual product screenshots wherever possible. Stock photos should be high-quality and not obviously generic.

2. **Consistency**: All assets should feel like they belong to the same brand. Maintain consistent color palette, typography, and visual style.

3. **Performance First**: Every image must be optimized. Large, unoptimized images will hurt SEO and user experience.

4. **Accessibility**: Ensure sufficient contrast, provide alt text, and include transcripts for videos.

5. **Mobile Consideration**: All images should work well on mobile devices. Test how hero images look when cropped for narrow screens.

6. **Dark Mode**: Consider how images look on both light and dark backgrounds. May need separate versions or overlays.

7. **Branding**: Subtle NTIGI branding in screenshots and graphics, but not overwhelming. Professional, not promotional.
